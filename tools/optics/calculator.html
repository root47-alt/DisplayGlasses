Eye box is how far the glasses can slide sideways (or up and down) on your face before the edges of the screen start to dim. Under 3 mm feels fussy; 5 mm or more is comfortable.

Focus range is how far the screen can move and still look sharp, from text floating at 0.5 m to text at infinity. The glasses module needs a slot about that long to fine-tune focus.

The model uses thin-lens optics and traces rays through the lens and splitter edges. Real lenses blur a little toward the edges, so treat the numbers as a guide and confirm them on the optics bench.
